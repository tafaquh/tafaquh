## 🧠 About Me

Engineering lead building **enterprise-grade SaaS platforms** focused on identity, access control, and secure distributed systems. Currently leading the end-to-end engineering organization at **portier Global** — reporting to the CEO, running a distributed team across Europe, Australia, Indonesia, and India, and driving platform reliability for enterprise clients worldwide.

Before stepping into leadership, I spent years as a backend engineer scaling high-traffic platforms across travel-tech (100M+ users) and edtech (22M+ users). I care about systems that are correct under pressure, teams that can execute across timezones, and the gap between technical excellence and measurable business impact.

- 🔭 Currently: Engineering Lead at [portier](https://portier.com)
- 🛠 Primary language: **Go**
- 📍 Based in Indonesia, working remotely across global timezones
